# Mosaic Monitor — Screenshots

[Home](../README.md) · [User guide](USER_GUIDE.md) · [Download](https://github.com/Rolandinfo-ui/mosaic-monitor-releases/releases/latest)

Real screenshots from Mosaic Monitor **1.0.24** on Android. The app supports **English and Hungarian**; these examples use English. Amounts, rates, sessions and dates are snapshots, not current values or a fixed offer. Android's system bars have been cropped; login/authenticator examples are focused crops of the relevant controls. Application content has not been recreated.

## Validator login and authenticator

The login flow includes a built-in TOTP authenticator. Once your credentials and authenticator are configured, automatic TOTP filling avoids switching to another authenticator app. Open Validator Login and confirm login to return to the node controls. These focused screenshots contain no saved personal credentials or authenticator account.
